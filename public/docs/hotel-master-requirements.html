01

Overview

Hotel Master provides boutique hotels and hospitality clients with a high-end showcase website. Visitors can explore technology integrations, amenities, service offerings, and submit consultation/booking requests directly via an Express backend integrated with Brevo transactional email delivery.

02

Stakeholders

HOTEL VISITOR / GUEST
Browses digital amenities, services, and submits booking or consultation inquiries.
HOTEL MANAGEMENT
Receives instant structured email notifications for booking and service inquiries.
